3. De ce folosim datele și pe ce temei legal

  • Furnizarea serviciului (cont, grupuri, înscrieri, lista de așteptare, echipe, rezultate, chat) – executarea contractului cu tine (art. 6 alin. (1) lit. b GDPR).
  • Echilibrarea echipelor pe baza notei de nivel – executarea contractului. Echipele sunt generate automat după note, dar organizatorii le pot modifica; aceasta nu este o decizie cu efecte juridice asupra ta în sensul art. 22 GDPR.
  • Emailuri de cont (confirmare, cod de autentificare, resetare parolă) – executarea contractului. Nu trimitem emailuri de marketing.
  • Securitate, prevenirea abuzurilor și jurnalul acțiunilor administrative – interesul nostru legitim de a proteja serviciul și utilizatorii (art. 6 alin. (1) lit. f GDPR).
  • Respectarea obligațiilor legale și apărarea unor drepturi în instanță – art. 6 alin. (1) lit. c și f GDPR.

4. Cine îți vede datele

  • Membrii grupurilor tale văd numele afișat, poza de profil, înscrierile, echipele, rezultatele și mesajele din chat ale meciurilor grupului.
  • Administratorii grupului văd în plus cererile de înscriere, nota de nivel (inclusiv autoevaluarea ta), prezența și statusul plății. Ceilalți membri văd doar media notelor pe echipă, nu notele individuale.
  • Poza de profil este stocată la o adresă web publică, greu de ghicit; oricine are linkul exact o poate vedea. Nu încărca o poză dacă nu dorești asta.
  • Adresa ta de email nu este afișată altor utilizatori.
  • Grupurile sunt pe bază de invitație; doar grupurile marcate explicit ca „listate” apar în căutare (numele și descrierea grupului, nu membrii).

Privacy Policy

Football Friends · Effective 30 October 2026. The Romanian version above prevails in case of differences.

1. Who we are

The controller of your data is Bogdan Petru Andrei, Bucharest, Romania (“we”). Contact: bogdan.andrei450@gmail.com. Football Friends is an app for organising friendly football matches: invite-only groups, first-come-first-served sign-ups with a waitlist, auto-balanced teams, chat and notifications.

2. Data we collect

  • Account: email, password (hashed only), account creation date; with Google sign-in, your Google account ID, name and email as sent by Google.
  • Profile: display name, optional profile photo, self-assessed skill level (0–5), language, notification preferences.
  • Groups: memberships, role, join requests, and the skill rating set by group admins.
  • Matches: sign-ups, waitlist position, team, scores, attendance, fee paid status (set by the organiser), Man of the Match votes, rating history used for team balancing.
  • Chat: messages you send in match chats.
  • Technical and security logs: IP address, browser/device type, sign-in times, log of admin actions in groups.

We collect no sensitive data, show no ads, sell no data and use no third-party analytics or tracking.

3. Purposes and legal bases

  • Providing the service (account, groups, sign-ups, waitlist, teams, results, chat) and balancing teams by rating – performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Teams are generated automatically but organisers can change them; this is not a decision with legal or similarly significant effects under Art. 22.
  • Account emails (confirmation, sign-in code, password reset) – contract. No marketing emails.
  • Security, abuse prevention and admin action logs – our legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)).
  • Legal obligations and legal claims – Art. 6(1)(c) and (f).

4. Who can see your data

Members of your groups see your display name, photo, sign-ups, teams, results and match chat messages. Group admins additionally see join requests, your skill rating (including your self-assessment), attendance and payment status; other members only see team averages. Your profile photo is stored at a public, hard-to-guess URL. Your email is never shown to other users. Only groups explicitly marked as listed appear in search.

5. Processors and transfers

Supabase (database, auth, storage, server functions – EU, Stockholm), Vercel (web hosting – global incl. US), Google (account emails via Gmail and optional Google sign-in – US/global). Transfers outside the EEA rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework (for certified providers) and/or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. Retention

Account and profile: until you delete your account. Sign-ups, teams, results and chat: while the match or group exists, and your own sign-ups and messages are deleted with your account. Admin action log: 12 months, keeping only an internal ID after account deletion. Backups: a short period set by the database provider. Provider technical logs: per their policies.

7. Deleting your account

In the app: Profile → Delete account. This deletes your account, profile, photo, sign-ups, messages, votes and ratings and removes you from all groups. Groups you own pass to an admin or the longest-standing member; groups with no one else are deleted. Lost access? Email bogdan.andrei450@gmail.com.

8. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability, objection to processing based on legitimate interest, and to withdraw consent at any time. Email us from your account address; we reply within one month. You may complain to the Romanian supervisory authority ANSPDCP (www.dataprotection.ro) or the authority where you live.

9. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and stored in the EU, protected by row-level access rules, hashed passwords and server-side operations. We will notify the authority within 72 hours of a qualifying breach, and you where the law requires.

10. Local storage and cookies

We only use strictly necessary local storage (sign-in session, language, pending invite). No analytics or advertising cookies.

11. Minimum age

The service is for people aged 16 or over.

12. Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date is shown above, and we will notify you of significant changes in the app or by email.
